First 30 Seconds

  1. Click an arrival or departure on the airport map.
  2. Assign it to `09/27` or `18/36` in the command panel.
  3. For arrivals: wait until the runway is open, then `Clear To Land`.
  4. For departures: queue them, `Line Up And Wait` if needed, then `Clear For Takeoff`.

Do Not Do This

  • Do not clear an aircraft if the runway card says it is occupied or resetting.
  • Do not launch a departure when an arrival already has that runway protected.
  • Do not over-hold everyone. Idle runways hurt efficiency and long waits hurt satisfaction.
